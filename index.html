Services Offered

Roam Veterinary offers three core surgical service lines, performed on-site at your clinic with the same standard of care you'd expect from a specialty hospital.

General Surgery

Routine and soft tissue procedures — laparoscopic spays, neuters, mass removals, exploratory surgery, and more — handled efficiently so your patients recover in a familiar space.

Laparoscopic Surgery

Minimally invasive options for spays and organ biopsies, offering smaller incisions, less post-op pain, and faster recovery for your patients — with improved diagnostic and patient outcomes.

Orthopaedic Surgery

For cranial cruciate ligament rupture, we perform Cranial Closing Wedge Osteotomy (CCWO) — a versatile, well-established stabilisation technique with outcomes comparable to TPLO.

Not sure which service line fits your case? Reach out and we'll talk it through together.

About Us

Roam Veterinary exists to close a simple gap: the surgical skill and confidence many primary practices don't yet have in-house. Serving Buckinghamshire and the surrounding area, we built a fully mobile surgical practice so that general practices can offer advanced surgical care — general, laparoscopic, and orthopaedic — without sending clients elsewhere or handing the case to a specialist hospital.

I'm an RVC (Royal Veterinary College) graduate who trained and worked in primary practice, and it was in that setting that I obtained my surgical certificate. As a certificate holder, Roam Veterinary is built around offering advanced surgical services designed specifically for the primary practice setting, closing the gap between routine general practice and full specialist referral.

While at my previous practice, I introduced endoscopy and orthopaedic surgery as services — experience that now underpins what Roam Veterinary offers on the road. I'm committed to evidence-based, high-quality surgical care, supported by ongoing professional development.

Our team brings hospital-grade equipment and surgical expertise directly to your clinic, working alongside your staff as an extension of your team, not a replacement for the relationship you've built with your clients.

Our approach:

  • We come to you, fully equipped for the procedure
  • We coordinate closely with your team before and after surgery
  • Your clinic stays the primary point of contact for the client
  • We treat every case with the same care as our own patients

Let's Talk About Your Next Case

Ready to refer a case, or want to learn more about how Roam Veterinary works with clinics like yours? Reach out — we'll walk you through scheduling, what to expect on surgery day, and how we handle follow-up care.

How it works:

  1. Send us the case — history, imaging and current weight.
  2. We send back a surgical plan, cost estimate and a suitable date.
  3. We come to you and carry out the surgery on site.
  4. Your team handles anaesthesia, recovery and follow-up, as usual.
